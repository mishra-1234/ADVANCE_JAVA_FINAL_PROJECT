// CAPABILITIES

Everything a security team needs

One platform to capture, investigate, and close the loop on every incident.

Incident Reporting

Submit detailed incident reports in minutes with a guided, structured form built for clarity under pressure.

Incident Tracking

Follow every case from open to resolved with live status updates and a full audit trail.

Evidence Management

Attach logs, screenshots, and files to each report, kept intact and traceable for investigation.

// PROCESS

From report to resolution

A fixed four-step path, so nothing sits unresolved.

01

Report Incident

An employee submits the incident with type, severity, and any evidence.

02

Security Team Reviews

Analysts triage the report and confirm its severity and category.

03

Investigation

The case is assigned and investigated until root cause is confirmed.

04

Resolution

The incident is closed, documented, and logged for future reference.
